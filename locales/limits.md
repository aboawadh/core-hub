# How much room each label has

Generated by `pnpm i18n:limits --measure` from the running web client (ADR 0028). Do not edit.

Each row is a web key shown in a label that holds **one line** and ends in an ellipsis (…) when its words do not fit. **Room** is the widest text the label shows, in CSS pixels, at the font size and weight given; ≈ Latin letters is that room in average Latin letters, a hint only. A translation may be as wide as the English, and as wide as the room; `pnpm i18n:limits` measures yours with real fonts and names every string that is wider than both. Shorter words, or a common abbreviation, are better than a cut label.

Keys that are not listed have no fixed room: they wrap onto more lines or the layout grows with them, and the pseudo-locale journeys check those screens.
