Core Hub is the Android app for your own Core Hub server: a self-hosted hub where your AI agents live, work and keep their memory. Talk to your agents, follow their work and approve what they want to do, from anywhere.

You need a Core Hub server of your own, or one that someone runs for you. The app connects only to the server you choose. Core Hub is open source: github.com/twuijri/core-hub

WORKS WITH HERMES AGENT
• A Hermes Agent app for Android: chat with Hermes, its skills, memory, jobs and channels, on the hub you run

CHAT WITH YOUR AGENTS
• Replies stream in as they are written, with Markdown, the tools the agent used and how long it thought
• Approve or deny an agent's action, and answer its questions, right in the chat
• Rooms: several agents in one conversation
• Attach photos from your gallery or camera, and files
• Dictate a message, and have replies read aloud
• Share text, links, pictures and files from other apps into a new chat
• English and Arabic, left-to-right and right-to-left

KEEP TRACK
• Every chat in one list, across your profiles, with search, pins and an archive
• The tasks board: see what each agent is working on, move a task, open its conversation
• Schedules: see what runs when, and run one now
• Notifications when a reply is ready or an agent is waiting for you

LOOK AFTER YOUR HUB
• Each agent's skills, MCP servers, memory, jobs, channels and plugins
• Profiles, users, models, knowledge, logs, usage and performance
• Pair another phone with a QR code

SIGN IN YOUR WAY
• Enter your server's address with your username and password
• Or scan the pairing code your hub shows on the web, or paste it

YOU DECIDE WHAT THE PHONE SHARES
• When an agent asks where this phone is, you choose each time, always or never; the answer goes only to your hub

Privacy: no account with the developer, no analytics, no ads. The app talks to the server you choose; notifications are delivered through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. The privacy policy has the details.
